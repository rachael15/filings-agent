# Apple Inc. (AAPL)

## Headline takeaways
- **Record June quarter:** revenue $109.4B, +16% YoY; diluted EPS $2.02, +29% YoY (earnings release).
- **Growth is broad:** every geographic segment grew double digits; Greater China and Europe each +22% (10-Q).
- **iPhone drives it:** iPhone $54.3B, +22%, attributed to Pro models; Mac +29%; iPad -6%; Services +12% to $30.7B (10-Q).
- **Margin flattered by one-offs:** gross margin 50.1% vs 46.5%, including ~2 pts from tariff refunds; EPS includes a $0.11 refund benefit (release).
- **Cost headwinds flagged:** management expects component supply constraints and cost increases (advanced semiconductors, NAND, DRAM) to *intensify* (10-Q).

Nine months: revenue $364.4B (+16%), operating income $122.4B (33.6% margin vs 32.1%), net income $101.5B. Annual history in the model: FY23 $383.3B, FY24 $391.0B, FY25 $416.2B revenue; FY25 operating margin 32.0%.

## Drivers and segments
- **Products gross margin** 40.1% (vs 34.5%) on mix and tariff refunds, partly offset by higher memory costs. **Services gross margin** flat at 75.6%.
- **Services** growth came mainly from advertising and cloud services (10-Q).
- **Opex** +23% YoY. R&D +32% to $11.7B on higher infrastructure-related costs, rising to 11% of sales from 9%. SG&A +10%.
- Effective tax rate in the quarter ~17.9% ($6.5B on $36.3B pre-tax); ~17.6% over nine months.
- **Capital return:** dividend of $0.27/share declared; share count down ~2% YoY (diluted 14.7B vs 14.9B). Cash and securities ~$146.5B vs term debt and commercial paper ~$84.3B at quarter end (release).

## Guidance and tone
The earnings release contains no numeric guidance. Tone is upbeat ("strongest June quarter ever", Cook; "record business performance", Parekh). The 10-Q is more cautious: gross margins "will be subject to volatility and downward pressure", and price increases may not offset cost pressure and could dent demand. **No call transcript was supplied**, so guidance given on the call is not reflected; add one with `--transcript`.

## What changed / key risks
- **Tariffs:** the Supreme Court struck down certain IEEPA tariffs (Feb 20, 2026); Apple applied for refunds, recognized as lower product cost of sales. New Section 301 tariffs were imposed, and a Section 232 semiconductor investigation remains open. Refunds are likely non-recurring.
- **Component costs:** memory and advanced-semiconductor tightness expected to intensify.
- **AI/software:** "Siri AI" and iOS 27 were announced at WWDC26; R&D spend is rising ahead of revenue from it.
- **FX:** tailwind in Europe and Greater China, headwind in Japan (weak yen).

## Questions to dig into
1. Q3 operating margin was 32.6% ($35.7B / $109.4B) and nine-month 33.6%. How much is tariff refunds, and what is the underlying run-rate?
2. How long can iPhone grow 20%+ — pricing, mix toward Pro, or a genuine upgrade cycle?
3. How much further does R&D intensity rise, and what is the payoff timeline?
4. How much memory cost inflation is contracted for the next 12 months?
